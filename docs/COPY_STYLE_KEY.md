# The Copy Style Key - the enchant-first register (R46)

Ruling R46, founder verbatim: "Lets try a blend with a beautiful and enchanting
way to explain first, with a simple explanation with tooltips throughout (so
one card may have 3 tooltips explaining 3 different parts of the module/etc)."

So the blend inverts from COPY-1: the SURFACE line enchants, in the register
of the founder's own writing, and the PLAIN one-sentence mechanics move into
tooltips. Sources, as ruled: joinseeds.earth, the second brain
(`regen-civics-clean/second-brain/`, chiefly `90 Voice Profile/` and
`08 Concepts/`), and regencivics.earth. Every rewrite in the Copy Book cites
one of the exemplars below by number.

## Exemplars, verbatim from the corpora

1. "What if healing ourselves and our Earth is actually a fun and Infinite
   Game?" - Voice Profile (the framing question, the spine of the project)
2. "Gratitude is a new way of distributing resources in this economy, through
   people just being grateful to each other for the contributions we bring."
   - Concepts/Gratitude
3. "The same gesture recognizes a contribution and moves the collective
   timeline." - Concepts/Gratitude
4. "needs woven together from the whole network into the center, the way
   mycelia weave a forest's roots into one web" - Concepts/Mycelia Governance
5. "Everyone here is here to do one of two things, which are really the same:
   heal and evolve." - Concepts/The Great Work
6. "no piece competes with another, each is needed for the picture to
   complete" - Concepts/Puzzle Pieces
7. "SEEDS is owned by no-one and designed for everyone." - joinseeds.earth
8. "Wherever you are, it's the perfect place to begin." - joinseeds.earth
9. "Unlike finite games played to win, infinite games are played to continue
   playing" - regencivics.earth
10. "the quests prepare us and get us started; the land projects push us"
    - Concepts/We Are the Land
11. "set the initial conditions, then hand the keys to the network"
    - Concepts/Network-Owned
12. "shifting our mindsets to see what's right and what we want more of"
    - Concepts/The Highest Timeline

## The sentence shapes he uses

- A short declaration, then the unfolding clause: "X is Y: and here is what
  that means for you." The colon lands the point (exemplars 5, 11).
- The definitional sentence with warmth: "Gratitude is a new way of
  distributing resources" (2), never a lecture.
- The mirrored pair, two halves of one truth: "the quests prepare us and get
  us started; the land projects push us" (10).
- The nature simile doing real explanatory work: "the way mycelia weave a
  forest's roots into one web" (4). The image explains the mechanism.
- Owned-by-everyone constructions: "owned by no-one and designed for
  everyone" (7).
- Direct address that lands where the reader stands: "Wherever you are, it's
  the perfect place to begin" (8).
- Load-bearing concepts capitalized as proper nouns: Game, Quest, Gratitude,
  the Infinite Game.

## Words he loves

co-create, weave, thread, seed, plant, root, grow, steward, gift, flow,
gather, abundance, alive, harvest, tend, bloom, mycelia, organism, timeline,
puzzle piece, the land, healing, evolve.

## Words he never uses (and the gate bans)

delve, tapestry, foster, leverage, vibrant, crucial, transformative,
testament to, beacon, unleash, seamless, robust, comprehensive, empower,
utilize, unlock-as-hype, navigate-as-metaphor. Also banned by the house gate:
em-dashes and en-dashes, contrast framing ("not X but Y", "rather than"),
rhetorical-question openers as filler, passive inspiration.

## The division of labor on every card

- The surface line ENCHANTS: short, warm, in the map's own register, an image
  that carries the mechanism the way exemplar 4 does.
- The tooltip EXPLAINS: one to three plain declarative sentences a first-time
  visitor trusts, no metaphor, the COPY-1 register. Up to three tooltips per
  card where three concepts genuinely live on it. Never a tooltip inside a
  tooltip.
